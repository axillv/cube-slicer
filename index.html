Cube Slicer

Cut the 3×3×3 cube into 27 separate 1×1×1 pieces with as few plane cuts as you can. Rearranging between cuts is allowed.

  • Slicetap a face to cut (on desktop, hover to preview then click). One plane through many pieces still costs one cut.
  • Movedrag a piece to restack it; use the nudge pad for exact steps. Drag the background or pinch to rotate/zoom.

Puzzle popularised by 3Blue1Brown, also known as Cubist Cuts. Code MIT. Built with AI-assisted coding (details).
