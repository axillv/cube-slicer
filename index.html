Cube Slicer

Cut the 3×3×3 cube into 27 separate 1×1×1 pieces using as few straight plane cuts as you can. You may rearrange pieces between cuts.

  • Slicehover a face to preview a cut plane, then click to cut. One plane through many pieces still costs one cut.
  • Movedrag a piece to restack it. Use the nudge pad or arrow keys for exact steps.

Puzzle popularised by 3Blue1Brown, also known as Cubist Cuts. Code MIT. Built with AI-assisted coding (details).
